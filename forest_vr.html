Forest Survival VR

Your plane crashed on a wooded island. Survive, craft, build, fight the cannibals.
Desktop: WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse look, click swing, E pick up, C craft axe, F campfire, B shelter, Z sleep (night, near shelter), 1 eat, 2 drink (at water)
Sandbox: M (Quest: left Y) opens the cheat menu. Arrows/right stick + Enter/trigger to pick. Something hidden is in there...
Quest: left stick move, right stick snap-turn, swing right controller to hit, right trigger pick up, right A axe, right B fire, left X eat, left Y shelter/sleep, left grip drink
